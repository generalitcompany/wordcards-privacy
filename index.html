Word Cards — Privacy Policy

In effect from: 20 October 2026

What we collect: nothing

Word Cards has no account, no sign-in and no server of our own. We do not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you or your child. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no social feature.

What stays on the device

So that it can work, the app saves a few things in its own storage on the device: the language chosen on the home screen, whether sound is muted, the best score for each game, and which words have been answered correctly or incorrectly so that they can be offered again.

None of this is ever sent anywhere. We cannot read it. Removing the app removes it.

Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details. Google Play tells the app only whether the Google account on the device owns the full version. Google's handling of that transaction is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

Spoken words

Words are read aloud by the speech engine already built into the device. Which engine that is, and whether it works without a connection, is a device setting outside this app. The app does not record audio and holds no microphone permission.

Permissions

The app requests internet access for one reason: Google's billing library needs it to reach Google Play. The app makes no network requests of its own.

Children

Word Cards is made for children. It carries no advertising, no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no links out of the app other than this page. The purchase sits behind a question only an adult can answer.

Deleting what is saved

Uninstall the app, or on Android use Settings → Apps → Word Cards → Storage → Clear data. Either removes everything the app has saved.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the app does, go to: support@generalitcompany.com
